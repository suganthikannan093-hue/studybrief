StudyBrief - AI Research & Study Companion
Built for the BharatCode Build League hackathon (https://bharatcode.ai/hackathon).
Tracks: Learning & education / Research & discovery.

Model API usage:
- All AI calls go to the BharatCode model API: POST https://bharatcode.ai/api/model/v1/chat/completions
- Auth: Authorization: Bearer $BHARATCODE_API_KEY (developer key from https://bharatcode.ai/account), kept server-side only.
- Default model: qwen-3.8-27b (configurable with BHARATCODE_MODEL).
- Output: structured JSON study brief validated with Zod, one repair retry.
Without a key the app runs a clearly labelled offline demo; no other model provider is used by default.
